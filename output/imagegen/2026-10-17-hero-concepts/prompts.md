# Pocketto hero image concepts

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. These are static selection concepts, not installed landing-page assets. The user requested three oil-painted variants of an AI agent walking along a map. No variant has been selected yet.

## Composition and decisions

- Portrait 2:3 composition gives the narrow hero column enough height to sit beside the long headline, body and installation block.
- Warm ivory, parchment, ink and burnt orange come from site/DESIGN.md and app/global.css.
- One walking agent following a route illustrates Pocketto's guided engineering workflow.
- Six checkpoints refer to Pitching, Grinding, Planning, Structuring, Development and Closing; they do not represent a live project state.
- No in-image text keeps all three assets readable at small display widths and avoids fabricated product claims.
- Oil brushwork follows the user's explicit style clarification.
- ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1: calm brand palette, winding route composition, static images.
- Cream edges are intended for the current light theme. Dark-theme adaptation and actual page fit must be assessed after selection.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: tall right-column hero illustration for the Pocketto engineering workflow landing page; a standalone raster asset, not a whole website screenshot.
Primary request: an AI agent visibly walking along a winding map route, translated into a sophisticated oil-painting UI illustration.
Composition/framing: portrait 2:3 canvas, ideally 1024x1536 pixels. Fill the tall composition from near the bottom to near the top with one connected legible map route and six sequential waypoints. The map occupies approximately 85% of the height and 85% of the width. Keep generous but controlled 7% safe margins. Make it read clearly when displayed about 450 pixels wide beside a long text column. No big empty upper half, no landscape framing, no collage, no multiple panels.
Subject: exactly one small original autonomous robot explorer, full body with two visible legs in a convincing walking stride, moving along the marked route around the lower-middle part of the map. A warm terracotta body and a small dark inset face panel; mature restrained character design, no exaggerated cute eyes, no antenna sparkle. The route and traveler should communicate a guided, accountable engineering journey through six checkpoints rather than random wandering. Checkpoints are understated physical map markers, not dashboard cards.
Color palette: use the existing site palette: warm ivory #faf8f3, ink brown #201b15, parchment #e8dfcc, one restrained burnt-orange #bd3d0a accent for the route and traveler. Tonal shifts within this palette only.
Backdrop: flat warm ivory #faf8f3 extending cleanly to all canvas edges so the image blends with a warm paper webpage. No rectangular frame, no dark border.
Text: none. No letters, labels, digits, captions, logos or watermark.
Constraints: painted map and embedded UI-inspired route wayfinding, no tables, no report/document panels, no browser chrome, no graphs, no terminals, no floating dashboard widgets, no neon, no blue-purple gradients. It must be unmistakably oil paint, with real visible brushwork, not smooth generic 3D. Avoid any landscape/map edge or character being unintentionally cropped.
Style/medium: VARIANT 1, ISOMETRIC IMPASTO. A tactile low-relief isometric route map, painted entirely in thick palette-knife oil paint. Restrained shallow layers of parchment terrain rise like topographic map contours, with a few precisely sculpted terrain ridges and broad calm negative space. A thick burnt-orange painted route winds in an ascending S from bottom foreground to upper background through six small circular checkpoint insets. The robot explorer strides on the route with one foot raised. Oblique aerial viewpoint around 35 degrees; monumental spacious forms, editorial art direction. Real impasto ridges catch soft upper-left light and cast only tiny grounded shadows. Material texture carries the interest; no unnecessary buildings or scenery, no decorative props. The map reads as a physical painted interface, with elegant alignment and intentional spacing, while remaining a painterly illustration.
```
