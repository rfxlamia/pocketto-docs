Each case study here is reconstructed from the files Pocketto leaves in a real repository: specs under `docs/pocket/spec/`, execution logs (`log.json`), per-task review reports, and `closeout.md`. Numbers come from those files. Where a plan is still open, the page says so.

Client names, internal feature names, and commit SHAs are removed. The engineering details are kept.
