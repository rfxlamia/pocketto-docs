`pocket-help` is the map of the Pocket ecosystem: what Pocket is, which skill to use right now, and how the local development flow works — without loading every skill into context.

**Core principle:** Orient cheaply, then load deep. This skill summarizes and routes; open an individual skill only when you reach the stage that uses it.

## When to use it

- You are new to Pocketto or unsure which skill fits.
- You ask "what is pocket", "how do I use pocket", "which pocket skill".

**When not to use it:** you already know the stage you are in — go straight to its skill.

## Prerequisites

None. This is the recommended first command after [installation](/docs/getting-started/installation).

## How to invoke it

```text
/pocketto:pocket-help
```

On Pi: "what is pocket", "how do I use pocket", or "pocket-help".

## How it works

It reads your question, classifies your situation, and routes:

- **Chained workflow** — the six [pipeline](/docs/pipeline) stages, for features and non-trivial work with reviewed handoffs.
- **Standalone workflow** — the everyday skills: `bug-hunting`, `hotfix`, `brand-design`, `structured-research`, `pocket-help`, `pocket-init`, `pocket-education`.
- **Optional adapter** — [pocket-enterprise](/docs/core-enterprise), only when you explicitly enable remote lifecycle synchronization.

Its routing rules of thumb, verbatim:

- Don't know if the problem is well-formed? → `pocket-pitching`
- Problem is clear but needs a real feature plan? → `pocket-grinding`
- It's a quick, well-understood change? → `hotfix`
- Something is broken? → `bug-hunting`
- Holding an unverified assumption? → `structured-research`
- Repo has Education on, or you say "teach me"? → `pocket-education` (the human implements)
- Explicitly enabling Enterprise? → `pocket-enterprise` (never inferred from credentials)

## Outputs and artifacts

None — orientation and a route. It also explains the end-to-end flow, the handoff facts (what auto-chains and what waits for you), and when the full pipeline beats a lighter flow: high ambiguity, many steps, BDD/TDD discipline, or phase gates and auditable handoffs.
